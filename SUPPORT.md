# Support

SoftConfig normally supports the latest two Minecraft versions relevant to Hypixel SkyBlock.

The initial transition treats 1.21.11, 26.1, and 26.2 as active targets. Active targets receive compatibility fixes, tested releases, and feature support.

Previously published artifacts remain available. Retained targets are maintained on a best-effort basis and are built regularly to make regressions visible, but they do not receive every new feature.

Support for an active target will not be removed without a documented deprecation notice. Existing artifacts and source history will not be deleted when a target leaves active support.
